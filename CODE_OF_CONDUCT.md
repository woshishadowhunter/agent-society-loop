# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
- Give and receive technical feedback with clarity and respect.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct them.
- Protect private information and credentials.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
